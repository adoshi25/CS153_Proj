You want to construct a highway in the middle of the city. How do we simulate this construction? What does the flow of traffic look like after this bridge is constructed? Which people are impacted? How can you make this as optimal and seamless of a process as possible? (So that it doesn’t take years)

People say that wars impact markets. But what areas? What times? Which stocks? With instability in Iran, traders should be able to see first hand (visually) how ships may slow down, how the supply chains are impacted, stalls in oil shipments, etc.

If you’re working in sustainability, you want to know how electric vehicles will impact society. What will emissions look like? How will adding forests affect different habitats? My bet is that these can be solved by using realistic simulation and agents that emulate human behavior on a day to day basis.
